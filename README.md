Roguelike Arena – Tactical ASCII Action & Survival!
Welcome to Roguelike Arena, a fast-paced, tactical rogue-lite experience built for action lovers and strategy enthusiasts alike! Step into a sprawling 8-level campaign filled with relentless enemies, explosive surprises, and high-stakes survival.

🔥 Key Features:
8 Challenging Arenas: Fight your way through diverse, procedurally generated levels, facing off against brutal grunts, ranged shooters, fast stalkers, heavy tanks, and the ultimate boss!
Dynamic Portal System: Cleaned an arena? Use the Blue Portal to advance deeper into the mission, or step back through the Red Portal to revisit previous floors and tie up loose ends.
Tactical Arsenal & Combat: Switch on-the-fly between versatile weapons (Pistol, Shotgun, and Railgun), manage your magazines, reload under pressure, and utilize tactical explosive barrels and throwable grenades to turn the tide.
Armor & Damage Mitigation: Equip Green and Red Armor sets designed to absorb a major percentage of incoming damage and keep you alive in intense firefights.
Endless Replayability (New Game+): Beating the final boss doesn't mean the end! Jump straight into New Game+ where enemy health, armor, and threat levels scale up for the ultimate survival challenge.
Interactive Environment: Open locked doors and search mysterious supply chests (C) to secure high-tier ammo, health, and gear.
🕹️ Controls Guide:
Movement: Arrow Keys or Numpad (1–9)
Aim Mode: SPACE (Move cursor and press ENTER to shoot or G to throw a grenade)
Look Mode: L (Inspect surroundings and enemy stats)
Weapons: 1 (Pistol), 2 (Shotgun), 3 (Railgun)
Actions:
W – Wait a turn
R – Reload active weapon
O – Open doors or supply chests
C – Close doors
Save/Load: F5 to Save, F9 to Load your adventure state.
Pause / Menu: ESC (Pause the game anytime and return with SPACE)
